How to Use Rhythm Wav Free

  1. Create a Playlist – Click "New Playlist" at the top. Give it a name and optionally upload a cover image (jpg/png).
  2. Add Songs or Videos – Use "Add New Song" or "Add New Video". Choose a playlist from the dropdown, then upload your file (mp3/wav for songs, mp4 for videos).
  3. Browse Your Library – All playlists appear as cards below. Hover to see the play button, or click anywhere on a card to select it for quick playback.
  4. Play Music – Selecting a playlist updates the "Now Playing" bar at the bottom — the music note spins when playing. Use the controls to play/pause.
  5. View Details – Click the "Open Playlist" link under any card to go to its full detail page (shows tracks/videos, individual play buttons, etc.).
  6. Free Limitations – 128kbps audio quality, ads after every 3–5 songs/videos, no offline downloads. Upgrade to Premium for full features!
