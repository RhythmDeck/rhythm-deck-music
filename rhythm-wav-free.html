How to Use Rhythm Wav Free

  1. Create a Playlist – Click "New Playlist" at the top. Give it a name and optionally upload a cover image.
  2. Add Songs or Videos – Use "Add New Song" or "Add New Video". Choose a playlist, then upload your file.
  3. Browse Your Library – All your playlists appear as cards below. Hover for play button, click to select for quick playback.
  4. Play Music – Selecting a playlist makes the music note spin. Use controls to play/pause.
  5. View Details – Click "Open Playlist" to see full track list, reorder, and play individual items.
  6. Free Limitations – 128kbps quality, ads every 3–5 items, no offline. Upgrade to Premium!
