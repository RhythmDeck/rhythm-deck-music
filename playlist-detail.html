How to Use This Playlist

  1. Switch Playlists – Use the dropdown at the top right to jump to another playlist instantly.
  2. Play Everything – Click "Play All" to start the whole playlist. Check "Play All in Order" if you want tracks in sequence (uncheck for random/shuffle).
  3. Shuffle Mode – The round black button with crossed arrows is the **Shuffle** button. Click it to toggle random playback order (it highlights when active). When enabled, "Play All" starts tracks in random order instead of sequence.
  4. Reorder Tracks – Drag and drop any track row to rearrange the order (changes save automatically in future versions).
  5. Play Individual Items – Click the play/video icon next to any track to play it alone. Songs show a simple alert (future: full player); videos open in a large popup.
  6. Watch Videos – Video tracks (marked with a video icon) open in a full-screen modal with controls. Close with the "Close" button.

Enjoy your curated music and videos!
