Finder's Fee Agreement

Parties: This Finder's Fee Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Rhythm Deck Music, located in Nashville Tennesse, US ("Company"), and the undersigned applicant ("Finder").

Recitals:

Finder may, from time to time, introduce artists, songwriters, producers, or other music industry opportunities to Company that result in executed recording agreements, distribution agreements, publishing agreements, licensing agreements, or other commercial transactions ("Qualified Transactions").

Compensation:

In consideration for such introductions leading to Qualified Transactions, Company agrees to pay Finder a finder's fee calculated as follows:

  • 6% of the first $100,000 USD (or equivalent) in advances, guarantees, or upfront payments received by Company from the Qualified Transaction.
  • 4% of the portion between $100,001 and $500,000 USD.
  • 2% of the portion between $500,001 and $1,000,000 USD.
  • 0.5% of any amounts exceeding $1,000,000 USD.

Fees are payable within thirty (30) days after Company receives the corresponding funds from the Qualified Transaction.

Term: This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of twenty-four (24) months from the date of execution.

Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Guanajuato, Mexico, without regard to conflict of law principles.

Entire Agreement: This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements.
