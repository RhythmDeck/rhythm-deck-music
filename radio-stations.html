Best Practices for Submitting Music

Commercial Stations

  1. The "One-Sheet": Always include a PDF with a short bio, high-res photo, and links to the music (Spotify/Soundcloud). Never send MP3 files as attachments unless requested.
  2. Follow Submission Guidelines: Most stations have a "Contact" or "Work with us" page. If they specify "No unsolicited submissions," try to build a relationship with the DJ of a specific specialty show first.
  3. Target "Specialty Shows": Many stations have a "Local Music" hour on Sunday nights. These are the easiest slots to get into as an independent artist.

College Stations

  1. Digital Assets: Send links to WAV or 320kbps MP3s. Do not send expiring links (like WeTransfer). Use Google Drive or Dropbox links that stay active.
  2. The "RIYL" Tag: Always include a "Recommended If You Like" (RIYL) list in your subject line or body. (e.g., Subject: New Indie Rock - BandName [RIYL: Tame Impala, Beach House]).
  3. Clean vs. Explicit: College stations are governed by FCC rules. You must indicate if a track is "Clean" or "Explicit." If there are "FCC Violations," provide a radio-edited version.
  4. Tracking: Send your music on a Tuesday or Wednesday. Follow up exactly two weeks later if you haven't heard back.
  5. Specialty Shows: Look at the station's schedule. If you play Metal, don't email the general MD; look for the "Metal Director" or the host of the specific show.

Professional Email Templates

Copy and customize one of these ready-to-use templates to pitch your music to radio stations. Replace placeholders in [brackets] with your details.

1. General Introduction (Clean & Professional)

Subject: New Music Submission – [Your Artist Name] – "[Song Title]"

Dear [Music Director's Name or "Music Director"],

I hope this email finds you well. My name is [Your Name], and I'm an independent artist from [Your Location].

I'm reaching out to submit my latest single "[Song Title]" for consideration on your station. It's a [brief genre description] that I believe would resonate with your listeners.

RIYL: [e.g., Bon Iver, Phoebe Bridgers]

Streaming links:
- Spotify: [Link]
- Apple Music: [Link]
- YouTube: [Link]

Download (320kbps MP3/WAV): [Permanent Dropbox/Google Drive link]

Attached is my one-sheet with bio, photo, and press links.

Thank you for your time and for supporting independent artists!

Best regards,
[Your Full Name]
[Your Artist Name]
[Phone Number]
[Website]
[Social Links]
