Submit your music to influential stations worldwide

Important Disclaimer

Radio station contacts, program directors, and submission policies can change frequently. Rhythm Deck works diligently to keep this database as accurate and up-to-date as possible, but we cannot guarantee that all information is current at the time of your submission. We strongly recommend verifying contact details directly with the station before sending your music. Rhythm Deck is not responsible for undelivered submissions or changes in station personnel.

Pro-Tip: For independent artists, "AAA" (Adult Album Alternative), College, and Non-Commercial stations are often the best entry points.

Professional Email Templates

Copy and customize one of these ready-to-use templates to pitch your music to radio stations. Replace placeholders in [brackets] with your details.

Template 1: General Introduction (Clean & Professional)

Subject: New Music Submission – [Your Artist Name] – "[Song Title]"

Dear [Music Director's Name or "Music Director"],

I hope this email finds you well. My name is [Your Name], and I'm an independent artist from [Your Location/Genre Focus].

I'm reaching out to submit my latest single "[Song Title]" for consideration on your station. It's a [brief genre description, e.g., "soulful indie-folk track with heartfelt lyrics"] that I believe would resonate with your listeners.

RIYL: [Recommended If You Like – e.g., "Bon Iver, Phoebe Bridgers, The Lumineers"]

Streaming links:
- Spotify: [Link]
- Apple Music: [Link]
- YouTube: [Link]

Download (320kbps MP3/WAV): [Permanent Dropbox/Google Drive link]

Attached is my one-sheet with bio, photo, and press links.

I'd love the opportunity to have my music featured on your station. Please let me know if you'd like any additional materials or a radio edit.

Thank you for your time and for supporting independent artists!

Best regards,
[Your Full Name]
[Your Artist Name]
[Phone Number]
[Website]
[Social Links]

Template 2: Local/Regional Focus

Subject: Local [Your City/State] Artist Submission – [Song Title]

Hello [Music Director's Name],

I'm a [your city/state]-based independent artist excited to share my new single "[Song Title]" with you.

As a local musician deeply connected to the [region] scene, I’ve been inspired by the great programming on your station and would love to contribute to it.

The track is [short description], perfect for [specific show or time slot if known].

RIYL: [3 comparable artists]

Links:
- Stream on Spotify: [Link]
- Full download: [Permanent link]

One-sheet attached.

Thank you for supporting local talent – I’d be thrilled for any airplay consideration!

Warm regards,
[Your Name]
[Artist Name]
[City, State]
[Contact Info]

Template 5: College Radio Pitch (RIYL Heavy)

Subject: New Indie Submission – [Artist Name] – "[Song Title]" [RIYL: Artist1, Artist2, Artist3]

Hello Music Director,

I'm an independent artist submitting my new single "[Song Title]" for playlist consideration.

It's a clean radio-friendly track (no FCC issues) with [short description].

RIYL: [3-4 comparable artists popular on college radio]

Streaming/Download:
- Spotify: [Link]
- SoundCloud: [Link]
- Permanent high-quality download: [Dropbox/Google Drive link]

One-sheet attached.

Thank you for supporting new music and college radio!

All the best,
[Your Name]
[Artist Website/Socials]

Tip: Always personalize the email with the recipient's name and reference their station/show when possible.
