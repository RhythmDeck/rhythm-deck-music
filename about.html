Richard (Ricci) Moore

Founder & CEO

Ricci is the CEO, Founder, and Creator of Rhythm Deck Music (rhythmdeckmusic.com), the all-in-one platform empowering independent musicians, songwriters, and producers to create, collaborate, promote, and monetize their music with professional-grade tools—including a built-in browser DAW, AI music video generator, live streaming, merch stores, and direct industry connections.

With over 20 years as a successful entrepreneur and independent contractor, Ricci has designed and built websites for clients across the United States, Canada, and Mexico. He has served as a trusted technical advisor, social media strategist, and digital marketer, helping businesses establish strong online presences and grow their audiences. His hands-on expertise extends to managing and training high-performing sales, customer support, and customer success teams for global brands including Sony Ericsson, Kellogg’s, Mabe, and Colgate. In these roles, he specialized in client onboarding, boosting retention rates, and implementing strategies that drive long-term loyalty and revenue.

Ricci has also worked directly with CEOs and top executives to elevate international awareness and adopt best business practices, bringing a global perspective and operational excellence to every project. Passionate about music himself, he is an indie amateur songwriter whose creative journey inspired the vision for Rhythm Deck—building a platform that solves the real-world challenges independent artists face every day.

In addition to leading the company’s strategic direction, Ricci personally built the entire front end of the Rhythm Deck website, delivering a sleek, intuitive, and fully functional user experience that puts creators first.
