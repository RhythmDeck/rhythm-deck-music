How to Use the Merch Admin Page

Welcome to your Merch Admin! Create and manage your custom merchandise store easily.

  1. Product Name & Price: Enter the name and your selling price for this item.
  2. Description: Write a compelling description to attract buyers.
  3. Image: Upload a high-quality main photo.
  4. Variants: Add sizes/colors. Each variant gets its own price (you set it directly).
  5. Save: Click "Add Product" or "Update Product".

Best Practices & Marketing Tips

  • Use clear, bright photos with multiple angles.
  • Write descriptions that tell a story (e.g., "Limited edition tour shirt").
  • Set competitive prices — factor in print costs + your profit.
  • Promote new items on social media and during live streams.
  • Offer bundles or limited editions to create urgency.
