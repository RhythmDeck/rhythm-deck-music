Instructions Manual

Complete guide to every feature in Rhythm Deck Studio

STEP 01

Transport, Tempo & Metronome

The large green Play button starts the transport. The Stop button immediately halts playback. Set your project tempo using the BPM box or by clicking TAP TEMPO repeatedly. Enable the Metronome to hear a timing click while recording or composing.

▶ ■ BPM
🎚️ Record Arm
STEP 02

Track Arming & Recording

Each track has a Record Arm button. Click it to prepare that track for recording. Armed tracks indicate readiness before transport playback. This workflow mirrors professional recording studios.

STEP 03

Mixer, Volume, Pan & Routing

Adjust track volume using faders with real-time dB feedback. Use the Pan knob to place sounds left or right in the stereo field. Mute silences a track, Solo isolates it. Reverb Send controls how much signal feeds into reverb processing.

🎚️ Pan • Mute • Solo
🎛️ Effects Rack
STEP 04

Effects Processing

Shape your sound using professional-style effects: EQ, Compression, Distortion, Delay, Reverb, Chorus, Saturation, Phaser, and Auto-Wah. Each slider updates in real time for immediate sonic feedback.

STEP 05

Timeline & Track Overview

The timeline displays each instrument track with animated visual feedback. Instructions beneath each track explain best practices for recording and layering. This helps guide composition from rhythm to melody.

🎼 Timeline
🎹 Piano Roll
STEP 06

Piano Roll Editor

Click to place notes on the grid. Drag notes to reposition pitch or timing. Resize note ends to change duration. Right-click (or long-press) deletes notes. Scroll horizontally to navigate longer sequences.

STEP 07

Drum Step Sequencer

Program drum patterns using the 16-step sequencer. Each row represents a drum sound. Click pads to toggle steps on or off. Active steps preview their sound instantly.

🥁 16-Step Grid
🎼 MIDI Keyboard
STEP 08

Virtual MIDI Keyboard

Use the on-screen keyboard to play notes with your mouse or touch. Ideal for testing melodies, sound design, or recording MIDI ideas without external hardware.

STEP 09

Loop Browser & Preview

Browse royalty-free loops by instrument and BPM. Click Preview to audition loops instantly. Loops play continuously until stopped, allowing hands-free comparison.

🔁 Loop Library
FINAL STEP

Export Your Mix

Click Export Final Mix to generate your full master output. Perfect for sharing, collaboration, or final production delivery.
