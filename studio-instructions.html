Instructions Manual

How to use Rhythm Deck Studio like a pro

STEP 1

Transport & Recording

Click the big green Play button to start the transport. Hit the Record button on any track to capture audio from your mic or guitar. Use the BPM display to set your tempo.

▶️ Transport + Record Buttons
Guitar Loop Bar
STEP 2

Looping & Overdubbing

Record a guitar riff, then click the loop icon on that track. Keep recording — each take layers on top (overdub). Perfect for building full arrangements.

STEP 3

Instruments & Guitar → Keyboard Mode

Click any instrument button (Guitar, Drums, Keyboard, Bass, Synth) to load a new track. Use Guitar → Keyboard Mode to record guitar and instantly hear it as piano/synth.

🎸 → 🎹
Effects Rack
STEP 4

Effects Rack (EQ, Compression, Distortion, Delay, Reverb, Chorus, Saturation, Phaser, Auto-Wah)

Drag any slider in the Effects Rack to shape your sound. Saturation adds warmth, Phaser creates movement, Auto-Wah reacts to your playing dynamics.

FINAL STEP

Export Your Mix

Click Export Final Mix to download stems + master mix. Share with your band or send to a producer.
