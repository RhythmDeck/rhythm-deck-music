Instructions Manual

Complete guide to every feature in Rhythm Deck Studio

STEP 01

Transport Controls & Recording

The big green Play button starts playback. The red square stops everything. Click the record arm button (red circle) on any track to arm it, then press Play to begin recording from your microphone or guitar input. Use the BPM display to set tempo — click "TAP TEMPO" 4 times or type the number directly. Toggle Metronome to hear a click track.

Loop + Overdub
STEP 02

Looping & Overdubbing

Record a take, then click the loop icon on that track. The section will repeat automatically. Keep recording — each new take layers on top (overdub). This is how you build full songs from a single instrument or voice.

STEP 03

Instruments & Guitar → Keyboard Mode

Click any instrument button to add a new track. The special Guitar → Keyboard Mode lets you record real guitar audio and instantly hear it transformed into piano, synth, or keys — perfect for quick layering without changing instruments.

Effects Rack
STEP 04

Effects Rack (EQ, Compression, Distortion, Delay, Reverb, Chorus, Saturation, Phaser, Auto-Wah)

Drag any slider to shape your sound in real time:
• EQ – boost or cut frequencies
• Compression – controls volume peaks
• Distortion – adds grit and edge
• Delay – creates echoes
• Reverb – adds space and depth
• Chorus – thickens and widens sound
• Saturation – gives warm analog tone
• Phaser – sweeping whoosh effect
• Auto-Wah – dynamic filter that reacts to your playing

STEP 05

Mixer & Volume Faders

Drag any fader left/right to balance volume (dB numbers update live). Pan knob (drag the white indicator) places sounds left or right in stereo. Mute/Solo buttons isolate tracks. Reverb Send controls how much signal goes to the reverb effect.

Virtual MIDI Keyboard
STEP 06

Virtual MIDI Keyboard

Click any white or black key on the on-screen keyboard to play notes. Use it to test melodies, record MIDI data, or trigger virtual instruments without a physical keyboard.

FINAL STEP

Export Your Finished Track

Click the bright Export Final Mix button to download individual stems + a full stereo master mix. Ready to share with your band, upload to streaming services, or send to a producer.
