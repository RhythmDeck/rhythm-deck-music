Instructions Manual

Everything you need to create professional music in your browser

STEP 01

Transport Controls & Recording

The big green Play button starts the transport. The red square stops everything. To record, click the record arm button on any track, then press Play. Your microphone or guitar input will be captured in real time. Set your BPM in the top right to match the tempo you want.

▶️ ■ Record Button
Loop Icon
STEP 02

Looping & Overdubbing

Record a take, then click the loop icon on that track. The track will now loop automatically. Keep recording — every new take layers on top (overdub). This is how you build full arrangements from a single instrument.

STEP 03

Instruments & Guitar → Keyboard Mode

Click any instrument button (Guitar, Drums, Keyboard, Bass, Synth) to add a new track. The special Guitar → Keyboard Mode lets you record a real guitar and instantly hear it transformed into piano, synth, or keys — perfect for quick layering without changing instruments.

STEP 04

Effects Rack

Drag any slider in the Effects Rack to shape your sound instantly:
• EQ – boost or cut frequencies
• Compression – controls dynamics
• Distortion – adds grit
• Delay – echoes
• Reverb – space & depth
• Chorus – thickens sound
• Saturation – analog warmth
• Phaser – sweeping movement
• Auto-Wah – dynamic filter that reacts to your playing

STEP 05

Mixer & Volume Faders

Use the faders on the left to balance each instrument. Drag left/right to lower or raise volume (dB numbers update live). Pan knobs place sounds left/right in the stereo field. Mute/Solo buttons let you isolate tracks while mixing.

Mixer Faders
FINAL STEP

Export Your Finished Track

Click the bright green Export Final Mix button. You will receive stems (individual tracks) and a full stereo mixdown ready to share with your band, upload to streaming, or send to a producer.
