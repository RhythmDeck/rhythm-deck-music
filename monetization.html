How Monetization Works

Maximize your earnings with Rhythm Deck's simple, artist-friendly monetization system.

Pro Plan Monetization

With the Rhythm Deck Pro Plan, you keep 90% of all tips and video sales, while Rhythm Deck retains 10%. This fair split allows us to keep Pro Plan pricing affordable at just $12.95/month (or less with annual discounts), so you can start earning immediately without needing to upgrade just for basic monetization.

All earnings from tips (during live streams & chats) and direct video sales go straight into your Stripe account. From there, transfer funds to your bank whenever you want — fast, secure, and with minimal fees.

Setup takes minutes • No hidden fees • Direct bank transfers

Platinum Pro Plan

Upgrade to Platinum Pro for just $60 extra per year (on top of your Pro Plan) and keep 100% of every dollar from tips and video sales — Rhythm Deck takes zero cut. This is the ultimate artist-first plan.

Private Merch Store – Sell & Earn Directly

Launch your own branded merch store inside Rhythm Deck with zero upfront costs. Sell t-shirts, hoodies, hats, posters, vinyl, stickers, and more — all printed on demand. You set your prices, keep 100% of profits after production costs, and we handle fulfillment & shipping.

No inventory • No risk • Direct payouts to your bank
