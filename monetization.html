How Monetization Works

Maximize your earnings with Rhythm Deck's simple, artist-friendly monetization system.

Pro Plan Monetization

With the Rhythm Deck Pro Plan, you keep 90% of all tips and video sales, while Rhythm Deck retains 10%. This split allows us to keep the Pro Plan pricing affordable at just $15/month (or less with annual discounts), so you don't need to upgrade to a higher tier just to access monetization features. We believe in empowering artists without adding extra costs for essential tools.

All funds from tips and sales go directly into your Stripe account once you've set it up through Rhythm Deck. From there, you can easily transfer earnings to your personal bank account. Setup is quick and secure, ensuring you get paid fast.

Platinum Pro Plan

For an additional $60 USD per year on top of your Pro Plan, upgrade to Platinum Pro and keep 100% of your tips and video sales. You'll also gain direct access to even more advanced features designed to supercharge your career.
