Music Video Scene Generator

Generate scenes and timelines for your music video based on lyrics.

How to Use:

  1. Generate Lyrics (Optional): Fill in a song concept, genre, and structure. Click "Generate Lyrics".
  2. Input Lyrics: Paste your lyrics into the text area.
  3. Add Video Details: Enter title and duration.
  4. Generate Scenes: Click "Generate Scenes From Lyrics".
  5. Review and Customize: Edit or add scenes.
  6. Generate Timeline: Click "Generate Timeline".
  7. Generate Images: Click "Generate Images".
  8. Download Scenes: Save as text file.
