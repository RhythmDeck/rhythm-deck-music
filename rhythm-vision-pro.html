Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Create Lyrics: Enter a song idea in "Lyrics Prompt", choose Genre, Number of Verses, and check Chorus/Bridge/Outro as needed. Click Grok, Gemini, or ChatGPT button — it copies a perfect prompt and opens the AI in a new tab. Paste the prompt there and generate lyrics.
  2. Paste Lyrics Here: Copy the generated lyrics and paste them into the large "Lyrics" box below.
  3. Generate Scenes: Click the purple "Generate Scenes" button. It will automatically create detailed visual scene descriptions for every part of your song (Verse, Chorus, etc.).
  4. Create Music: Choose Voice Gender, Voice Tone, and select Instruments. Click the orange "Generate Music (MP3)" button. Wait 30–60 seconds — your full song will play when ready.
  5. Generate Images: Click the green "Generate Images" button. It will create 6 professional photos for each scene (you can change the number per scene).
  6. Create Video: After music and images are ready, click the large cyan "Generate Full Music Video (MP4)" button. A demo video will appear (real video creation coming soon).
  7. Download: Use "Download MP3" for your song and "Download MP4" for the video when available.

Tip: Start with Step 1 and follow the numbers — everything works in order!
