Creating Your Rhythm Deck Account & Getting Started

Creating Your Rhythm Deck Account (Sign Up Page)

This guide walks you through creating your Rhythm Deck artist account, choosing a plan, and setting up your unique artist page.

1. Getting Started

To begin, go to the Sign Up page on Rhythm Deck.

You’ll see:

  • A brief introduction at the top
  • A “Create Account” button
  • A sign-up form where you enter your details

Click Create Account or scroll down to the form.

2. Filling Out the Sign-Up Form

Name / Band Name

Enter:

  • Your artist name or
  • Your band name

Tip: Use the name you want industry professionals to recognize.

Email Address

Enter a valid email address.

This email will be used for:

  • Logging in
  • Account notifications
  • Collaboration messages

Make sure you use an email you check regularly.

Password

Create a secure password.

Requirements:

  • At least 6 characters
  • Something you’ll remember

You can click “Show” to see what you’re typing.

3. Choosing Your Subdomain (Very Important)

This step creates your personal artist URL on Rhythm Deck.

What is a subdomain?

Your subdomain becomes your public artist page address.

Example:

yourname.rhythmdeckmusic.com

How to choose a good one

  • Use lowercase letters
  • No spaces
  • You can use hyphens (-)
  • At least 3 characters

Good examples

  • retro-rock-band
  • dj-nightwave
  • sarahbeats

Bad examples

  • My Band Name
  • !!!music!!!
  • a

As you type, Rhythm Deck automatically checks availability:

  • ✅ Available! → You can use it
  • ❌ Taken → Choose another name

Once chosen, this subdomain is yours, so pick carefully.

4. Selecting Your Plan

Choose the plan that fits your goals. You can upgrade later.

Available Plans

  • Free Plan
    Basic access • No payment required • Great for exploring the platform
  • Rhythm Vision Pro
    Music video generator • Choose monthly or yearly access
  • Rhythm Vision Platinum
    Unlimited video creation • Commercial rights • Priority processing
    Best for artists releasing content regularly
  • Pro Plan
    Full Rhythm Deck platform • Artist tools and collaboration features
  • Platinum Pro Plan
    Everything in Pro • Plus radio station database and merch tools and store
    Best for serious career growth

After choosing a plan, you may be asked to select a billing duration (monthly or yearly).

5. Agreeing to Terms

Before creating your account:

  • Check the box to agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy

This is required to continue.

6. Creating Your Account

Click Create Account.

What happens next depends on your plan:

  • Free Plan
    Your account is created instantly • You’re taken directly to your artist dashboard
  • Paid Plans
    Your account is created • You’re redirected to a secure payment page
    After payment, you’ll be sent to your dashboard automatically

You’ll see a confirmation message when everything is successful.

7. After Sign-Up

Once logged in, you can:

  • Complete your artist profile
  • Upload content
  • Explore tools based on your plan
  • Start collaborating on Pro and Platinum plans

Your artist page will be live at:

your-subdomain.rhythmdeckmusic.com

Helpful Tips

  • Take your time choosing your subdomain — it’s part of your brand
  • Use a professional email
  • You can upgrade or change plans later
  • If payment succeeds, you’ll be redirected automatically
