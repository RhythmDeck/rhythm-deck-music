Terry Sweet: Days of Dyanna

Days of Dyanna - A memory and a mystery. Dyanna was not my wife’s name but is the mythical version of her. Days of Dyanna follows the life long relationship journey and a life that ended tragically. It’s about secrets that she never shared - but I knew anyway.

Emmy-nominated composer, song writer, singer, pianist Terry Sweet has composed hundreds of radio and TV jingles such as Kellogg¹s Raisin Bran, Kraft, John Deere and many more winning many Addy Awards, Silver Microphones and Telly Awards. Terry has produced in studios across Nashville, Chicago, New York, Dallas, Salt Lake - working with musicians/singers from such bands as Faith Hill, Brooks and Dunn, Neil Young, Crosby Stills and Nash, Blood Sweat & Tears, Chicago, Amy Grant and many others.

Q&A

What is Rhythm Deck?

Rhythm Deck Music is the ultimate destination for musicians, songwriters, bands, vocalists, producers, and music lovers of all genres to connect, share, and grow.

Whether you’re creating original songs, producing beats, or shooting your own music videos, Rhythm Deck makes it easy to upload, share, and connect with an audience that truly appreciates the art of sound.

Our mission is to empower independent creators with tools to shine — without the noise or limitations of mainstream platforms. Here, your music takes center stage. Join us, unleash your creativity, and be part of the next revolution in music sharing.

Is it really free?

Yes! The Free Plan includes some creative tools and public uploads. The Platinum Pro Plan offers a full range of tools and is in pre-launch. For a limited time, any artist who signs up for our pre-launch special will have and keep Platinum Pro for life for FREE!

What do I get with Pro?

Pro unlocks sharing tools, fan polls, live streaming, and eligibility for Main Stage & Featured Artist slots.

How do I upgrade?

You can upgrade anytime from your profile admin page or during signup.

How do I get on the Main Stage or Featured artist?

Artists can apply or be nominated. Criteria: originality, quality, engagement, and visuals. Final approval by staff. Updated monthly.

Can I collaborate with other artists?

Yes! Connect with others in the Rhythm Deck community to collaborate on music and visuals.

Can fans interact with my music?

Pro users can host polls, Q&A, and fan shoutouts directly from their artist page.

Are there limits to uploads?

Free users have limited file sizes, while Pro users get higher storage and priority uploads.

When are new features added?

We release new AI and community features monthly based on user feedback.

Can I monetize my content?

Pro users will soon be able to earn from fan tips and sponsorship integrations.
